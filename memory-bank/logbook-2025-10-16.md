Notes / troubleshooting excerpt (issues encountered & resolved)
- CSE requests require RVI (X-M2M-RVI) and RI (X-M2M-RI). Without these, the CSE returned 501/400 errors. The mood service now supplies RVI/RI/Origin for reads/writes.
- Subscriptions: enc.net and nct must be compatible. Used `enc: {"net":[3]}` with `nct:1` for notification content types accepted by the CSE.
- CSE subscription verification can fail if the CSE cannot reach the target (`nu`), which we observed when trying to create certain subscriptions; demo script handles create/check and cleans up.
